# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss concrete behavior and evidence, welcome questions, and make criticism useful to the recipient.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Keep conversations relevant to the project and respect maintainers' decisions about scope and participation.

Contributors can disagree about architecture, code, and priorities. Explain the tradeoff, respond to evidence, and avoid personal attacks. Attribution and correction should be clear and fair.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close discussions, restrict participation, or remove access in response to violations. They should apply these measures consistently and protect the privacy of people reporting concerns.

Report conduct concerns to a maintainer through an established private contact channel. Security vulnerabilities use [the security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
