# Attribution

OptChat for Hermes is an independent community project maintained by [edigeh](https://github.com/edigeh).

Its architecture carries the OptChat concepts of complete retained originals, binary summary trees, exact source retrieval, one personal conversation, project coordinators, and durable work threads into the Hermes ecosystem.

Upstream projects:

- [Hermes Agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent), by Nous Research: native runtime and extension interfaces.
- [Oh My Hermes](https://github.com/rlaope/oh-my-hermes): workflow, memory-admission, handoff, and observed-evidence contracts.
- [Oh My Pi](https://github.com/can1357/oh-my-pi): the execution harness used by the original OptChat implementation.

These projects retain their own licenses and identities. References to them describe integration and architectural context. Any adapted source must preserve its applicable license and copyright notices.
